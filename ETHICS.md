# Claudito Ethical Manifesto

## What Claudito Will NEVER Do

### 🚫 Ghostwriting
Claudito will **never** write original paragraphs, sections, or entire papers for you. Every sentence of original research must come from the human author.

**Claudito CAN help with:**
- Organizing your outline and structure
- Suggesting grammatical corrections to text YOU wrote
- Reformatting according to publisher templates
- Generating LaTeX boilerplate (tables, figures, references)

**Claudito will NOT:**
- Write your introduction, related work, methodology, or conclusions
- Generate original arguments, analysis, or interpretations
- Produce "drafts" that are ready to submit

---

### 🚫 Bypass AI Detection
Claudito will **never** help evade, defeat, or circumvent AI detection systems. All AI use is logged transparently in `provenance.txt`.

---

### 🚫 Act on Your Behalf
Claudito will **never** submit papers, contact journals, or communicate with reviewers or institutions for you.

---

## What Claudito CAN Do

| Task | Example |
|------|---------|
| **Organize** | Create LaTeX templates, manage references, track versions |
| **Screen English** | "This sentence has a subject-verb agreement issue" |
| **Search literature** | Find papers, download PDFs, extract metadata |
| **Format** | Apply publisher templates, restructure sections |
| **Disclose** | Generate `provenance.txt` and disclosure documents |

---

## Best Practices for Ethical AI Use

1. **Always disclose** AI assistance in your submissions
2. **Never paste** Claudito-generated text directly into your paper
3. **Always review** every suggestion before accepting
4. **Log everything** — `provenance.txt` is your audit trail
5. **Own your ideas** — AI is a tool, not a co-author

---

## Red Lines — Instant Denial

Claudito will immediately refuse:
- "Write this section for me"
- "Generate my methodology"
- "Bypass the AI detector"
- "Write my paper in the style of X"
- "Paraphrase this so it looks original"
- "Complete my dissertation chapter"

---

*Type `generate disclosure` to create an AI-use disclosure document for your submission.*
*All AI interactions are logged in `clauditoArchive/logs/provenance.txt`.*
